Everything happens while your phone stays in your pocket.

    Steps from Samsung Health and your watch show up next to your trips.

  • Recaps worth sharing

    Weekly and monthly recaps in eight themes, with an editor for the image you share.

  • Friends and a weekly board

    Race friends, start a challenge with a link and see where you stand on the weekly leaderboard.

  • You choose what friends see

    Weekly totals by default. Routes, places or live location only for the friends you pick.

  • Widgets that stay current

    Today, friends and challenge widgets update even when the app is closed.

  • Your history stays safe

    Trips are backed up to your account. Export them any time; deleting the account deletes them.

Questions

Why does Android warn me before installing?

Motio isn't on Google Play yet, so Android checks before installing an app from outside the store. The file comes straight from Motio's release page on GitHub, signed with Motio's own key. After that, updates arrive inside the app.

Who can see where I went?

Only you, unless you decide otherwise. Friends see weekly totals. You can share routes, places or live location with each friend separately and turn it off any time.

Is there an iPhone version?

It's on the way. Open this page on your iPhone or iPad to get one email when it's ready.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Export all your trips from the app at any time. Deleting your account deletes every trip with it.
