motio How to install

Every move tells a story.

Motio notices when you walk, run, ride or drive, draws your day as a road, and turns your week into stories worth sharing.

Download for Android Free. Needs Android 8 or newer.

Your day, drawn for you.

Keep your phone in your pocket. Motio records each trip on its own and lays them out as one road from morning to night, with your time on the move, steps and distance.

It knows how you moved.

Your phone's motion sensors and Google's activity recognition tell a walk from a run, a ride from a drive. Fix a trip once and Motio learns it for next time.

Walk Run Ride Drive Transit

Race the people who keep you moving.

Add friends with a link, compare your weeks and start a challenge: first to 30 km, most active minutes, or the most runs. Cheer each other on when someone pulls ahead.

Every friend gets a page.

Streaks, all-time distance, longest outing and favourite way to move, plus your record against each other. Keep your own profile private whenever you like.

Your routes stay yours.

Friends see weekly totals, never where you went. Your trips live in your account, you can export them any time, and deleting your account deletes all of it.

Install in a minute.

  1. 1

    Download the app

    Tap the button below on your Android phone.

  2. 2

    Allow the install

    Android asks once whether your browser may install apps. Allow it, then tap Install.

  3. 3

    Open Motio

    Create your account and allow location and activity, so trips are recorded on their own.

Why does Android warn me?

Motio isn't on Google Play yet, so Android asks before it installs an app from outside the store. The file comes straight from Motio's release page on GitHub, signed with Motio's own key, and has no ads.

After that, new versions arrive inside the app: tap Update when it asks.
