Your routes stay yours.

Friends see weekly totals, never where you went. Your trips live in your account, you can export them any time, and deleting your account deletes all of it.

Install in a minute.

  1. 1

    Download the app

    Tap the button below on your Android phone.

  2. 2

    Allow the install

    Android asks once whether your browser may install apps. Allow it, then tap Install.

  3. 3

    Open Motio

    Create your account and allow location and activity, so trips are recorded on their own.

After that, new versions arrive inside the app: tap Update when it asks.
